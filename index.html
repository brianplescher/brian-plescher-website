About

Who is Brian Plescher?

What does Brian Plescher write?

Books and essays on transformation, collapse, and the practice of thinking clearly in systems that are quietly failing. Author of Woundwise (critical theory) and The Directed Author (creative leadership).

What is Brian Plescher's background?

M.S. in Management, Strategy, and Leadership from Michigan State University. Executive Education from Yale School of Management. Founder of Sonexial, where I build AI-ready author websites.

What genre is Brian Plescher's work?

Critical theory, philosophy, and creative leadership. Writing that bridges academic rigor with practical application for readers navigating uncertainty.

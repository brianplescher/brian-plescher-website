Now

Updated July 2026.

Building

Sonexial. I’m building Sonexial — a company that builds AI-ready author websites and marketing infrastructure for self-published writers. Most of my current energy is going into refining the offer, writing the anchor content, and landing the first clients.

Writing

Between Sonexial builds, I’m drafting essays and working on notes toward the next book. I write on weekends and in the margins.

Reading

I’m reading around the edges of philosophy, infrastructure, and the politics of attention — with a particular eye toward what it means to build durable work in unstable conditions.

Thinking about

The relationship between authorship and infrastructure, and what it means to make work that remains legible and alive when the platforms around it are designed for extraction rather than care.

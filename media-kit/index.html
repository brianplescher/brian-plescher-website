Bio

Brian Plescher is a Grand Haven, MI-based author, strategist, and founder of Sonexial. He holds an MS in Management, Strategy, and Leadership from Michigan State University and executive education from Yale School of Management. Brian is the author of Woundwise and The Directed Author, where he explores creative leadership, transformation, and the future of authorship. Through Sonexial, he helps authors and organizations build resilient, future-proof platforms. Connect with him at brianplescher.com.
